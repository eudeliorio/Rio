ABOUT Rio

We're building the FX infrastructure Latin America runs on.

One platform for fintechs and enterprises to convert, move, and hold money across Latin America. FX is live today; payments and virtual accounts are in open beta, expanding across the region.

01 Why we exist

Latin America deserves first-class financial rails.

Companies operating across the region still stitch together brokers for FX, processors for payments, and banks for accounts, a different stack in every country and none of them built with LatAm as the priority. Stablecoins changed what's possible: programmable dollars that move at internet speed, convertible into local currency. Rio provides that liquidity, with cross-border payments and virtual accounts now in beta alongside it, so one relationship can replace the whole patchwork.

02 The products

Three products. One platform.

01 · Live

FX & Liquidity

The foundation, and where Rio is strongest: sharp spreads and deep stablecoin liquidity against MXN, COP, PEN and USD, with BRL, CLP, ARS, CRC, DOP and GTQ rolling out next.

02 · Beta

Cross-border & local payments

Moving money in and out of Latin America, including to and from third parties. In beta on the US ↔ Mexico corridor today, with new corridors opening as our currency coverage expands.

03 · Beta

Virtual accounts

Stablecoin wallets and USD virtual accounts, in beta with clients today, with LatAm local-currency accounts coming soon. Hold balances, convert on your schedule, and pay from one wallet.
